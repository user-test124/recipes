
## Ingredients
- Round rice  
- Sesame oil  
- Red cabbage  
- Carrots  
- Sesame seeds  
- Soy sauce  
- Rocket salad  
- Ginger  
- Lime  


## Steps
- Rince well the rice  
- Cook rice with just enough water for ~10min with the lid  
- Let the rice sit, add sesame oil and soy sauce, mix gently  
- In a bowl, grate the carrots  
- Add the red cabbage cut into thin slices  
- Add also the ginger cut in small dices  
- Pour some lime juice over the mix  
- Add the rice to the bowl once cold, stir again  
- If needed, add more sesame oil or soy sauce  
- Finally add the sesame seeds and rocket salad
